Privacy

This site sets no cookies and runs no advertising or tracking scripts.

If you use a contact form, what you type is sent once, through our own handler on Cloudflare, to the Ryeton team’s inbox. We do not keep it in a database and do not add you to any mailing list. You can also write to hello@ryeton.com from your own email.

What you send us is read only by the Ryeton team, used only to reply to you, and never shared or sold. Financial information you share later is covered by a confidentiality agreement signed before you send it.

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, write to hello@ryeton.com.
