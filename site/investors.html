How we plan to run each firm

Software and AI prepare the routine work. A manager reviews it, approves it and stays responsible. Each year fewer routine steps need a person, so good managers spend their time on boards and buildings.

Software prepares

Board packages, payment matching, vendor invoices, work orders and routine resident questions.

A manager decides

Reviews, approves and answers for the result. Urgent and sensitive matters always go to a person.

Every approval leaves a record

Approved work posts to the firm’s existing accounting system with a full trail.

Why the model compounds

Boards stay when service is steady

Co-op and condo boards rarely switch firms that serve them well. Keeping the same managers protects that.

Each firm makes the next one easier

Finance, insurance, hiring and IT are shared across the group, and every workflow we automate works at the next firm too.

Small firms are overlooked

Large consolidators rarely buy firms of this size, so sellers have few good options and we are not bidding in auctions.
